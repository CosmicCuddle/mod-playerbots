# Custom Modifications Register

## PB59-AUTOGEAR-CAPPED-EXECUTOR-20261121 — IP-capped command → equipment

- **Owner/component:** CosmicCuddle / `mod-playerbots`.
- **Parent/source reference:** CS01 DRAFT PR43 `e055efc8` on Grok PR37 `cefa1d9d` (both unchanged). Coordination [#59 C1](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/59#issuecomment-7061116709); DRAFT PR #44.
- **Intended behavior:**
  - **Gearing:** with `Playerbots.AutoGearIPCap.Enable`, the queued world-thread autogear/bis command now gears the bot under the commanding human's IP stage cap and the stricter score limit.
  - **One boundary:** every command form resolves through a single `ResolveCommand` path.
  - **Random bots:** gear above the cap moves to empty bag slots first.
  - **Alt bots:** never stripped, and reset is refused.
  - **Nothing destroyed:** nothing the bot owned is deleted. Items the command created above the cap are revoked.
  - **Config tables:** the IP cap tables can only lower the approved caps.
  - **Max-level commanders** go through `ResolveTieredAutoGearRoute`. At Vanilla stages 1-7 the cap is min(equipped average, stage cap). At TBC/Wrath the average is also bounded by the worn raid tier, which is the era floor (T4 125 / T7 213) unless the G7 raid source index, bound to the commander's exact capture, proves a higher tier. BiS keeps the stage cap.
  - **G7 source index:** built once on the world thread at startup, only when the IP cap is on (about 5 s on the scratch DB), and published immutable. Each table is counted with the same SQL first; a failed count or query, a short result or a malformed row (including an `INT32_MIN` loot reference) publishes nothing. Signed world columns are read the way Core reads them.
  - **Raid source gate (C1-B):** the factory, BiS overlay and finishers never offer an item whose loot shows a raid when every source is a raid the commander cannot enter yet. A spawned dungeon or world drop, an open raid, a vendor, or a reachable quest clears it. A quest is locked while a hand-in or start item, a required creature, or the previous quest in its chain is locked (owner instruction 22 Nov, quoted in [#74](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/74#issuecomment-7079237461), e.g. the Zandalar "Paragons of Power" rewards wait for Zul'Gurub). The required stage is Individual Progression's own entry gate; Zul'Gurub and Zul'Aman use IP's config keys. Without a loaded index the command keeps the gear.
  - **Crafts and items without a source (owner instruction 22 Nov, [#74](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/74#issuecomment-7079669889)):** items no loot, vendor, quest or craft row names are refused at every stage. Crafting is a source: what spells create and teach comes from the loaded spell data (Spell.dbc with `spell_dbc`, SkillLineAbility.dbc); who teaches them comes from `trainer_spell`, `item_template` spells (recipes and use spells) and quest reward spells. A trainer or skill-learned recipe is reachable; a recipe item, the item whose use creates it, or a quest reward spell follows that item or quest (Zul'Gurub recipes and the Edge of Madness charms wait for Zul'Gurub). Recipes nobody can learn and creation loops are refused. A quest hand-in without a source does not lock the quest.
  - **Script-summoned raid bosses:** bosses without a `creature` row (Nefarian, Ragnaros, Ouro, Hazza'rah, Gahz'ranka and TBC/WotLK script bosses) are placed at the map and difficulty of the encounter they credit (`instance_encounters` creditType 0 + DungeonEncounter.dbc). Their loot is then raid evidence: Head of Nefarian and Master Dragonslayer's Medallion wait for BWL (stage 2). On the Core53 world DB 86 bosses are placed; the single-raid classifier used by the tier lift then proves 2,370 raid items instead of 1,730 (mostly TBC/WotLK).
  - **Vendors, reputation and quest requirements ("yes if they fit the criteria", [#74](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/74#issuecomment-7079669889)):** a vendor row counts only when the vendor stands somewhere open (an unspawned vendor is no evidence either way) and every item its ExtendedCost asks for (ItemExtendedCost.dbc) is obtainable. An item with `RequiredReputationFaction` is locked, whatever its source, while every known way to gain that reputation (`creature_onkill_reputation`, positive quest reputation) is locked; the rank is not compared. A quest is also locked by its `RequiredMinRepFaction` and by the items its hand-ins are made from (`ItemDrop1-4`); a quest that needs a reputation is no way into it; an exploration or scripted-event quest (`SpecialFlags` 2) neither opens nor locks anything. Example: Zandalar reputation comes only from Zul'Gurub (kills, coin and bijou hand-ins, Channeler's Heads), so Rin'wosho's Bloodvine patterns and the Bloodvine set first appear at stage 3. Measured on the Core53 world DB: 120 equippable items up to ilvl 66 are refused by the raid gate at stage 0 (77 without these rules), all Zul'Gurub, AQ or Onyxia related; 922 have no source; the common Vanilla reputations (Argent Dawn, Timbermaw, Cenarion Circle, Thorium Brotherhood, Hydraxian Waterlords) stay open.
  - **Freshness:** the index is the world database at startup. Core's `.reload` of loot templates, `npc_vendor`, `quest_template`, `creature_template` or trainers, and GM-added spawns, reach AutoGear only after a restart (Core offers modules no hook after a reload). Staleness can go either way (a removed vendor still counts as a source), so restart after such changes. Count and rows are separate pool queries with no shared snapshot: a changed row count publishes nothing, a same-count edit during the startup window is not detected.
  - **Random-bot gear (owner instruction 22 Nov, quoted in [#74](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/74#issuecomment-7079237461); activation not approved):** with `Playerbots.RandomBotGear.RaidSourceGate` (default 0), gear the factory gives random bots on creation, re-randomize and level-up follows the same gate at the bot's own IP stage (highest rewarded marker quest 66000 + stage). Refused items per stage are precomputed at load. Alt bots and humans are unchanged; worn gear is not removed. Without IP or a loaded index random-bot gear is unchanged, and the server logs a startup error saying so (owner instruction, [#74](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/74#issuecomment-7079669889)).
  - **Inventory safety (P0, [#52](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/52#issuecomment-7118592534), gates A03-A07):** a strip plans its own empty bag slot for every item (`PrepareEquipmentStorage` with a slot mask, off-hand first) and runs all-or-nothing through the storage transaction runner: exact slot facts (`CapturePB59InventorySlot`) are checked around every move, earlier moves are undone in reverse order, and an outcome the runner cannot prove is reported as needing recovery, never as a rollback. Each move reads the item again after the unequip script hook and must get the same item back from `StoreItem`/`EquipItem`. A BiS restore that fails stops the run; owned items that leave the inventory are counted; success is whispered only when gearing ran, the cap held and nothing needs recovery. The factory checks a replacement again after the old item took a bag slot, so Core never mails a displaced off-hand (the old item goes back instead). This also applies to the factory outside AutoGear. Without the Core slot-facts header a strip is refused. The new refusal lines have English fallbacks only; the AutoGear texts have no translation SQL yet.
- **Affected files:** `src/Bot/AutoGearCapCommand.h`, `src/Bot/AutoGearCappedExecution.h`, `src/Bot/AutoGearCommandRoute.h`, `src/Bot/AutoGearSourceIndexStore.h/.cpp`, `src/Bot/AutoGearSourceRowLoad.h`, `src/Bot/AutoGearSourceAccess.h`, `src/Bot/AutoGearMasterSourceIndex.h` (source map per entry, additive), `src/Bot/Factory/PlayerbotFactory.h/.cpp` (one cap helper; optional filter, and the random-bot filter only when its option is on), `src/PlayerbotAIConfig.h/.cpp`, `src/Ai/Base/Actions/TrainerAction.cpp`, `src/Script/Playerbots.cpp`, `conf/playerbots.conf.dist` (comments), `tests/pb59_autogear_capped_command.cpp`, `tests/pb59_test_autogear_adapter_contract.py`, `tests/pb59_autogear_source_access.cpp`, `tests/pb59_autogear_source_row_load.cpp`, this Register.
- **Dependencies:** Core `TryReadPB59AutoGearProgression` / `CapturePB59Equipment` (Core PR56 `c9dced25`) and the IP provider `c1725f84`.
- **Tests:** `pb59_autogear_capped_command` 269 (the fake inventory runs the real transaction runner with failures at every move position, failed rollback, lost bot context and a replaced item), the adapter source contract with 18/18 negative controls, `pb59_autogear_source_access` 180 and `pb59_autogear_source_row_load` 93 checks pass on GCC/Clang/ASan; all 46 standalone pb59 suites that build without a Core tree pass. The Core53 integrated worldserver compiles with 0 warnings, and the dry-run gate is clean with the feature off and on. No in-game command has been run.
- **Live status:** IP cap, random-bot raid gate and AutoGear OFF; not merged or deployed.
- **Rollback:** keep `Playerbots.AutoGearIPCap.Enable = 0` or revert the branch; no persistent data is changed.

## PB-F14-SOCIAL-DISPATCH-20261114 — world-thread social dispatch and RandomBot adoption guard

The existing queued `GroupInviteOperation` also receives the standard group-invite veto before its direct group creation/conversion/add. It captures both participant epochs to reject stale queued work. This preserves ordinary IP grouping enforcement for permitted personal/Altbots without classifying them as F14 social RandomBots. Same-session invite-policy changes after preflight and native/gameplay behavior remain unverified.

Lifetime audit: public Core `395b68ee` waits for map workers in `MapMgr::Update` before the PB world-operation drain, so the map-AI logout path is serialized at that pin. The installed Core and other exceptional teardown paths remain unverified; the generation lock itself does not protect Player/session lifetime.
The Player destruction hook retires login and invitation generations as an idempotent fallback if a higher-level logout path was skipped.

- **Owner:** CosmicCuddle. **Owning component:** `mod-playerbots` in its own repository.
- **Parent/source reference:** public `CosmicCuddle/mod-playerbots` master `405f81196161017eebae3ee7a612d786a3ed2a9d`; branch `feature/codex-pb-f14-social-dispatch-20261114`. Installed Playerbots/Core/IP versions are UNKNOWN.
- **Intended behavior:** bot-originated group invite, trade initiation and battleground port packets enter their session queues for world-thread handling. Group accept runs as one world-thread operation, with its existing master/strategy/summon follow-up only after the handler has established membership. The operation captures non-reusing bot and inviter login generations plus a distinct invitation generation, rejecting queued work from a previous login or invitation cycle. Personal-bot `add`, `login`, `addaccount` and the nonzero-master `AddPlayerBot` entry refuse positively identified type-1 RandomBot-account characters; own/linked alts and type-2 AddClass accounts retain their prior path. Missing character account or unresolved managed-account type fails closed.
- **Affected files:** `src/Ai/Base/Actions/InviteToGroupAction.cpp`, `AcceptInvitationAction.cpp`, `TradeAction.cpp`, `AcceptBattlegroundInvitationAction.cpp`; `src/Script/WorldThr/PlayerbotOperations.h`, `PlayerbotIdentityGeneration.h`; `src/Script/Playerbots.cpp`; `src/Bot/PlayerbotMgr.cpp`; `tests/f14_social_dispatch_source.test.js`; this Register and `docs/F14_SOCIAL_DISPATCH_CANDIDATE_2026-11-14.md`.
- **Dependencies:** pre-sync Phase-A Core23 `dbb1dd5150f330d88522a10b85e93546fff72bad` packet and hook behavior plus the isolated Core #30 v2 guild seam `8a306898bfa4dd5ee7ef5195e0b539ba05824799`; existing Playerbots `WorldSession` packet queues, `PlayerbotWorldThreadProcessor`, type-1/type-2 account assignments, character cache and source-matched IP social hook. Upstream-stage Core #28 v2 is separate. No new config, SQL, DBC or client patch.
- **Known risks:** queued packets may outlive their original action; Core revalidates the target state at drain, but only staged tests can show end-to-end behavior. The group-accept operation rechecks both login epochs, the invite, session and membership at execution. Pinned Core supplies an invitation callback, now used to distinguish same-session, same-group reinvite cycles at source/model level; staged gameplay remains unverified. A logout racing after the epoch check needs native validation. An unavailable account-type snapshot can block a managed account command. The other direct unsafe Playerbots routes remain outside this change.

- **Live status:** DRAFT only, NOT MERGED and NOT DEPLOYED. F14/Paid remain OFF.
- **Decision:** owner-approved RandomBot non-adoption policy in [coordination Issue #13](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/13#issuecomment-6867335447); CS01 accepted the four-route dispatch candidate in [Issue #13](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/13#issuecomment-6866003156) and assigned this lane in [Issue #51](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Naxxramas-Server-Coordination/issues/51).
